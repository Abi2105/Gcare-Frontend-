About Us

Objective

The underprivileged people dwelling in rural areas should make use of the top medical facilities through virtual mode and get consultation from medical experts duly post-diagnosis

Mission

Every human being living in remote villages should avail the facility on health care services and get benefitted through virtual link on par with the multi-specialty medical services available in cities.

Vision

To make our citizens, especially people dwelling in rural areas, free from ailments through integrating medical expertise and advanced technologies by creating a virtual platform to interface between medical experts and patients to avail a comprehensive service, post-diagnosis.
