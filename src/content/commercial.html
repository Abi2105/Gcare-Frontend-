Commercial Module

The client’s needs to buy kiosks as one-time purchase as the as per their own investment as it can be also loan through any financial institutions (Nationalized Bank, NBFC Funding agency & funding corportion) etc. In this the G-care will be only a supporting partner. the whole investment has been carried by the investor.

Revenue sharing is the distribution of the total amount of income generated by the sale of good or services between the stakeholders or contributors... As with profit shares only the profit. (Buyer) will have invest 30% ofoverall. The revenue sharing will be 60%-40%

The Renting the Kiosks the other parties (buyer) has to me invest 20% of the entire amount along with added conditions the investor at least have to buy minimum 25 kiosks on first purchase.

The G- care has offered the franchisees module for Kiosks to the other parties (buyer) where they have to invest 60% and G-care will invest 40% along with marketing strategies to be approved from G-care community guidelines.

Social Domain

The rental social module has been specially designed to give boost to Government projects like Ayushman Bharat Yojna, Pradhan Mantri Bharatiya Janaushadhi pariyojana) so that it can also serve the society.


The G-care is facilitating social module for kiosk which come along below mention conditions to be fulfilled. After incorporating the social module, the other parties (buyer) will have to serve at least 20 patients (mandatory) daily for free of charge.


The investments derived for the social module will be later reimbursed by the G care Diabetic council through local NGO after the verification representative from local NGO (approved by G-Care Council) will be send for verification
